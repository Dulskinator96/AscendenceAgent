Treat successive correction passes as dependent observations. Use the task or other prespecified independent unit for uncertainty. Do not select the most favorable measure after seeing results; label exploratory analyses. Show negative/null findings, failures and tradeoffs. Statistical significance alone is not practical significance. Agreement alone does not establish truth.

## Ready to proceed when
Analysis is independently inspectable, primary claims have explicit evidence and limitations, and the team agrees what conclusions the study does and does not support. If evidence is insufficient, document that result and revisit the protocol rather than force a publication claim.
