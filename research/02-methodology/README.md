## Candidate case format
Record a stable case ID, exact task, source/version and relevant passages, allowed variation, intended failure mode, and independent expected claims/criteria. Keep gold labels and held-out materials outside model-visible inputs; do not publish restricted data. Define before/after criterion identity so gains and damages are comparable. No universal numerical threshold is presumed.

## Ready to proceed when
Human collaborators approve and version the protocol, review data/ethics permissions, establish independent evaluation, and decide whether formal preregistration or institutional review is appropriate before data collection. Any later changes must be dated and distinguished from confirmatory analysis.
