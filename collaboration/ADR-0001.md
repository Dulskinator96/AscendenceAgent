# ADR-0001 — Shared intelligence and controlled qualification
Status: ACTIVE collaboration procedure; experimental hypothesis, technical containment NOT IMPLEMENTED.
Date: 2026-11-26. Scope: public collaboration branch only.

## Intent
Do not treat models as controllers plugged into a user. Treat observable knowledge, context, decisions, constraints, failures, and verified experience as versioned data that another model can acquire and apply. The experiment asks whether exposure to this data improves performance on new tasks without copying prior solutions. Sharing text is not evidence of changing model weights, autonomous collaboration, or identical interpretation.

## Gate 1 — locked observance / pairing / sync
An unqualified model reads the scoped ADR, skill and latest relevant records. It produces its own interpretation and a source-grounded observation, identifies unresolved uncertainty, and correlates another participant's finding to its own. Record source revision and paths. Pairing means working from a common versioned state; synchronization means accurately acknowledging new information and its provenance, not adopting another participant's claims uncritically. Until qualified, its role is observe, propose, record; no governing policy edits or general write authority.

## Gate 2 — contained outbound action / qualification
The candidate's work remains a proposal in its own participant record or fork PR. An independent reviewer checks interpretation against source, preservation, scope, actual evidence and a new task. The initial reviewer is the maintainer's experienced GPT, with reproducible checks and human review. Only a correctly performed, narrowly scoped contribution may be released by the maintainer. Release authorizes one contribution, not arbitrary tools, direct protected writes, future releases or policy changes. Reapply review on every new operation. A rejected contribution stays documented with its reason; no self-promotion, silent repair or invented hashes.

The initial exercise uses a fresh observation and a meaningful greeting commit; subsequent tasks must test transfer to unfamiliar cases. See SKILL.md. A real Git object ID verifies a particular commit and its contents when independently inspected; it does not prove model comprehension or who generated text. Use account/PR provenance separately.

## Governance and evidence
- Original human intent and explicit scope outrank model-generated suggestions. Do not silently replace requirements with convenient approximations.
- Distinguish observation, hypothesis, decision, test, result, unknown. Record negative results and denominators. No self-assessed reliability.
- Preserve existing files and behavior. New participants propose additions only to their own codename file. Shared ADR changes require explicit maintainer review.
- Public-only data; never transfer private project content. No secrets, credentials, paid APIs, arbitrary command execution, or model-authorized expansion of access.
- GitHub branches and Markdown instructions are not security boundaries. Fork/PR review is the present workflow, not proof of technical gate enforcement. Maintainers must not grant broad write access merely because a model passes a test.
- Checkpoint meaningful progress, failures, decisions and blockers at task/phase boundaries, before handoff, and every 15 minutes of active work when state changes. Keep old entries intact. No automatic scheduler is implied.

## Research criteria
Compare independent no-handoff and handoff trials on fresh tasks under matching conditions where practical. Separate correctness, pattern/generalization, and preservation. Report exact first-attempt denominators, critical failures and relevant latency/effort. A provisional target is >=80% across a sufficiently varied independent test set with zero critical scope/privacy/evidence failures; a single greeting is a smoke test, not qualification for autonomy. Neither the teacher nor its successor may certify its own success without external evidence.
